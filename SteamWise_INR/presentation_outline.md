# SteamWise Presentation Outline

## Slide 1 — Title
SteamWise: Steam Game Recommendation & Price Analysis System

## Slide 2 — Problem
A large game catalogue makes it difficult to compare titles and find games within a budget.

## Slide 3 — Objectives
- Clean and explore Steam game data
- Recommend games from preferences
- Analyze prices and ratings
- Present results with charts

## Slide 4 — Technology
Python, Pandas, NumPy, Streamlit, Plotly.

## Slide 5 — System workflow
CSV → preprocessing → standard columns → filters → weighted ranking → UI and charts.

## Slide 6 — Recommendation formula
Genre/tag preference 45%, rating 30%, review strength 15%, price fit 10%. Explain that the weights are heuristic.

## Slide 7 — Live demo
Dashboard → Game Recommender → Price Analysis → Game Explorer.

## Slide 8 — Limitations
Historical dataset, missing values, no live sale-price API, heuristic rather than trained model.

## Slide 9 — Conclusion
SteamWise makes game catalogue analysis and preference-based discovery interactive and explainable.
